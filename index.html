Aral Deck Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Aral Deck respects your privacy. This policy explains how the application handles your information.

Aral Deck is an offline-first study application. No account registration or login is required.

2. Information Stored

The app stores deck names, descriptions, flashcard questions, answers, hints, notes, review history, and study progress.

This information is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device.

3. Information Sharing

The app does not operate an online database or transmit study materials to the developer or third parties through its current app implementation.

The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. Device Backup

Android may back up eligible app data depending on your device settings and backup configuration. Such services are managed by Android and your backup provider.

5. Data Security

Study information is stored in the app's private internal storage. Android's application sandbox helps protect it from access by other applications.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete study content using the app's available features. Removing the app or clearing its data may also delete locally stored information. Backup copies may be subject to your device's backup settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The app does not require account information and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through an online service.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the app's features or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
